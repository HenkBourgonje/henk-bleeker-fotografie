A century ago, a remarkable group of people settles in the wilderness of Paraguay: the Mennonites. The closed society grows into a very successful farming community with strong traditions that are passed down from generation to generation.

Artist and photographer Henk Bleeker from Alde Leie gets the rare chance in 2025 to get to know the closed community up close. In black and white he shows who these remarkable people are and how they, in their very own way, hold on to their own traditions and faith.

**Menonitas** takes you to a world that seems far away, but feels surprisingly close. A world that invites you to pause at questions that still matter today. What do we keep of our traditions? What connects us to each other? And what can we learn from people who consciously choose a simpler life?
