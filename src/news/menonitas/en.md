In 2025 Henk Bleeker photographed a closed Mennonite community in Paraguay. His portraits and landscapes tell of togetherness, identity and traditions.

In Menonitas, photographer and artist Henk Bleeker from Alde Leie offers a look into the lives of the Mennonites in Paraguay. In 2025 he got the rare chance to get to know this closed community up close.

Over the past century the community built a successful farming society there. Faith, togetherness and simplicity shape daily life, while traditions are passed down from generation to generation. Bleeker's portraits and landscapes show how the people live and who they are.

The exhibition invites you to think about identity and belonging. What do we keep of our traditions, and what can we learn from people who consciously choose a simpler life?

17 October to 6 December 2026, Museum Martena, Franeker.
