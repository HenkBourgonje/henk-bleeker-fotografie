---
title: Havana Moon
---

I'm watching a boxing match between Arnold Vanderlyde and the Cuban Felix Savon. It's 1992 and I'm taking in that year's Olympic Games. Vanderlyde loses. I wonder how it is that, all these years, so many Cubans have dominated the boxing world. I had always been fascinated by footage of the ultimate boxing legend Muhammad Ali. Strong poses, dramatic light, beautiful black and white technique. A few years later I was in Cuba for a bikini shoot for the brand Cyell. When that was done I went to have a look at the boxing gyms in Havana, and what I found there was, to my eyes, stunning. I started shooting in black and white with one goal: I wanted to smell the sweat that had to splash off the prints.
