---
title: Icons of the West
---

In a borrowed camper I'm touring the Midwest, in the land of Uncle Sam. A while back I came across a book by a photographer who had captured the raw cowboy life in a poetic way. So this era still exists, I want this too, I must have thought, that was in 2002. It began in the one horse town of Elko NV. For many years this would be my starting point in my search for the Old West, in one of the most rural areas in America. I visited a lot of working ranches there and became one of the boys. The Icons with a lasso, me with a camera.
