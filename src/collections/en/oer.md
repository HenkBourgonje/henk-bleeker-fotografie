---
title: Oer
---

The photo series ‘Oer’ by Henk Bleeker is of megalithic proportions.

His imposing images of various dolmens in Drenthe are about the mystery of these ancient places of power. How could people build these structures 5000 years ago? Were they burial places or spiritual meeting places where the worship of woman for her fertility, new and previous lives and death came together? Let us marvel.
