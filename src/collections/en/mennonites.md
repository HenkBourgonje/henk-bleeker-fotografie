---
title: Mennonites
---

A century ago, a remarkable group of people settles in the wilderness of Paraguay: the Mennonites. This closed society grows into a highly successful farming community with strong traditions passed down from generation to generation. While the world around them changes faster and faster, they choose to hold on to a rhythmic life in which faith, togetherness and simplicity are central.

In 2025, artist and photographer Henk Bleeker from Alde Leie is given the rare chance to get to know this closed community up close. His photographs show not only how the Mennonites live, but above all who they are. Behind every portrait and every landscape lies a story of connection, identity and the strength of tradition.

### Who are the Menonitas?

The history of the Mennonites begins closer to home than you might think. Their faith arose in the 15th century with the Frisian reformer Menno Simons (1496–1561). After a long journey through Europe, his followers eventually ended up in places such as Paraguay, where they have managed to preserve their own culture to this day.

Menonitas takes you into a world that seems far away, yet feels surprisingly close. A world that invites you to pause on questions that still matter today. What do we keep of our traditions? What binds us together? And what can we learn from people who consciously choose a simpler life?
